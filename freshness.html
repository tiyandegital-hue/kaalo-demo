Is your bag in the window?

Coffee needs rest, then it has a peak. Enter the date stamped on your bag — this is the same maths we use at the roastery, not a marketing version of it.

The three states of a bag

No bag is “fresh” forever, and a just-roasted bag isn't ready either. Honest version:

Days 0–6
Resting Still releasing gas from the roast. The cup tastes sharp and thin — this is why this week's batch isn't on sale yet.
Days 7–21
In the window The peak stretch. Everything the lot has to say, it says now. We plan dispatch so your bag lands at the start of this window.
Day 22 +
Past peak Still drinkable, honestly — but flatter week by week. Ground coffee fades faster; whole beans keep a little longer.
